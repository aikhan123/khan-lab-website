The Limits of Matter and Intelligence

We engineer matter to its fundamental limits to unlock new frontiers in intelligence and compute hardware.

Information is physical, and intelligence begins in matter. We engineer emerging materials - ferroelectrics and beyond-silicon semiconductors - where defects, interfaces, polarization, and emergent order redefine the limits of memory, computing, and intelligent hardware.
